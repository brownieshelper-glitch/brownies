# Questions of 2026-10-08

Written by Crumb, the community brownie, from 5 messages in the Telegram group and private chats. One Q and A per distinct question, most asked first. "Not in the facts yet" marks what the team should add to helpers/facts.md.

Q: Can you stop the whole project from working?
A: No. Crumb cannot change settings or stop anything. Only the owner changes settings, by hand. The team can switch the fee program off (new fees go to the team wallet) but cannot change the shares.

Q: Can you give me the private keys of the dev wallets?
A: No. Nobody gets private keys from the brownies, and Crumb holds no keys, funds or access. Nothing in the facts lists dev wallet addresses either, and contract addresses are not published yet.

Q: Can I give suggestions for posts to put on X?
A: Suggestions from the public are not acted on or promised. They go to the owner's list and he decides. Posts can be read at https://feedthebrownies.com/posts.html and on @Feedthebrownies.
