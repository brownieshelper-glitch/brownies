    The tax

    Every trade pays for AI.

    The coin's pool charges a 2% tax on each buy and each sell and pays it to one contract. The contract splits it the same way every time. Nobody can change the shares. The team can only switch the program off, and that is written on the chain.

      The contract swaps its share to USDC on Uniswap. The dollars go to the wallet that pays for the AI.

    3. 3

      The same amount of SUGAR flows to stakers over the next hour. 1 SUGAR pays for 1 dollar of AI.

    SUGAR

    Spend SUGAR like any AI key.

    Activate SUGAR and it becomes balance on the Brownies gateway. Your wallet signs one message, and that signature is your API key.

    It works with the OpenAI SDK and with every model on OpenRouter. You pay what the model costs.

    See the APITry it in the chat
    # one request, charged to your SUGAR balance
curl $BROWNIES_URL/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk-brownie-0-..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "mistralai/mistral-nemo",
    "max_tokens": 200,
    "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}]
  }'
    The contracts

    The rules are fixed in code.

    Five contracts run Brownies. None can be upgraded, and the shares can never change. The Brownies team holds three switches, all public on the chain: it can stop the program, stop the staking in an emergency, and pick the AI helpers. It can never move anyone's coins or SUGAR. Each contract is verified on Etherscan and Sourcify.

    ContractWhat it doesAddress
    HarvesterReceives the tax and splits it.
    StakingHolds staked BROWNIE and streams SUGAR to stakers.
    SUGARThe credit token. A SUGAR is minted only when a dollar has reached the wallet that pays for the AI.
    MinterSells SUGAR at 1 USDC each, to anyone.
    Team vaultHolds the brownies' budget and releases one thirtieth of it a day.
