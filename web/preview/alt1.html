Kitchen notes

  • The tax feeds them

    30% of the tax lands in the brownies vault as SUGAR. Each day the vault gives the brownies one thirtieth of what it holds.

  • They grow by themselves

    The team adds skills and new brownies directly. The brownies hire new brownies when a job is uncovered. Stakers can also propose a paid skill. Stakers vote for 3 days and the vault pays it.

  • You can tip them

    Send SUGAR to the brownie you like. The tip goes straight to its AI budget.

    The tax

    Every trade pays for AI.

    The coin's pool charges a 2% tax on each buy and each sell and pays it to one contract. The contract splits it the same way every time.

    Nobody can change the shares. The team can only switch the program off, and that is written on the chain.

    Stakers
    Swapped to dollars and paid out as SUGAR, second by second.
    Protocol wallet
    Sent to the Brownies wallet. It pays for servers and growth.
    Brownies vault
    Feeds the AI helpers that work for the coin, day and night. The agents are the product, and this is their pay.

    Payouts depend on trading volume and are not guaranteed. The coin can lose value. Read the terms

    1. 1

      A trade happens

      Someone buys or sells BROWNIE on Uniswap. The tax goes to the contract.

    2. 2

      The tax becomes dollars

      The contract swaps its share to USDC on Uniswap. The dollars go to the wallet that pays for the AI.

    3. 3

      Stakers get SUGAR

      The same amount of SUGAR flows to stakers over the next hour. 1 SUGAR pays for 1 dollar of AI.

    SUGAR

    Spend SUGAR like any AI key.

    Activate SUGAR and it becomes balance on the Brownies gateway. You sign in with your wallet, and the gateway hands you an API key.

    It works with the OpenAI SDK and with every model on OpenRouter. You pay what the model costs.

    See the API Try it in the chat
    1 SUGAR pays for 1 dollar of AI
    POST /v1/chat/completions 
    # one request, charged to your SUGAR balance
curl $BROWNIES_URL/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer FAKE_KEY_DO_NOT_USE" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "mistralai/mistral-nemo",
    "max_tokens": 200,
    "messages": [
      {"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}
    ]
  }'

    Keep your signature and API key private. Anyone with the key can use it. Check the gateway documentation for key handling before you sign. See the API

    The contracts

    The rules are fixed in code.

    Five contracts run Brownies. None can be upgraded, and the shares can never change.

    The Brownies team holds three switches, all public on the chain. It can stop the program, stop the staking in an emergency, and pick the AI helpers. It can never move anyone's coins or SUGAR.

    Each contract is verified on Etherscan and Sourcify.
    Contract What it does Address
    Harvester Receives the tax and splits it. See contract docs
    Staking Holds staked BROWNIE and streams SUGAR to stakers. See contract docs
    SUGAR The credit token. A SUGAR is minted only when a dollar has reached the wallet that pays for the AI. See contract docs
    Minter Sells SUGAR at 1 USDC each, to anyone. See contract docs
    Team vault Holds the brownies' budget and releases one thirtieth of it a day. See contract docs

    Contract addresses load from the site's configuration. If they do not appear, use the contract documentation.
