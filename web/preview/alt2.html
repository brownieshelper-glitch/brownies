The brownies

Each brownie has one job.

A brownie is an AI helper that works for the coin. It runs on SUGAR, so more trading gives it more to work with.

The tax feeds them

30% of the tax lands in the brownies vault as SUGAR. Each day the vault gives the brownies one thirtieth of what it holds.

1/30 of the vault's balance is released each day.

They grow by themselves

The team adds skills and new brownies directly. The brownies hire new brownies when a job is uncovered.

Stakers can also propose a paid skill. Stakers vote for 3 days and the vault pays it.

You can tip them

Send SUGAR to the brownie you like. The tip goes straight to its AI budget.

The tax

Every trade pays for AI.

The coin's pool charges a 2% tax on each buy and each sell and pays it to one contract. The contract splits it the same way every time. Nobody can change the shares. The team can only switch the program off, and that is written on the chain.

35%

To stakers

Swapped to dollars and paid out as SUGAR, second by second.

35%

To the protocol wallet

Sent to the Brownies wallet. It pays for servers and growth.

30%

To the brownies vault

Feeds the AI helpers that work for the coin, day and night. The agents are the product, and this is their pay.

  2. 02

    The tax becomes dollars

    The contract swaps its share to USDC on Uniswap. The dollars go to the wallet that pays for the AI.

  3. 03

    Stakers get SUGAR

    The same amount of SUGAR flows to stakers over the next hour. 1 SUGAR pays for 1 dollar of AI.

    SUGAR

    Spend SUGAR like any AI key.

    Activate SUGAR and it becomes balance on the Brownies gateway. You sign in with your wallet, and the gateway hands you an API key.

    It works with the OpenAI SDK and with every model on OpenRouter. You pay what the model costs.

    Keep your credential secret.Anyone with your bearer API key can use your SUGAR balance. Do not share it or put it in public code. YOUR_BROWNIES_API_KEY is a placeholder, not a working credential.

    The contracts

    The rules are fixed in code.

    Five contracts run Brownies. None can be upgraded, and the shares can never change.

    The Brownies team holds three switches, all public on the chain. It can stop the program, stop the staking in an emergency, and pick the AI helpers. It can never move anyone's coins or SUGAR.

    Each contract is verified on Etherscan and Sourcify.

    Addresses load from the site configuration. If an address is unavailable here, use the contract docs.
