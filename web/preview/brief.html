A new version of the home page: "A Kitchen With Receipts"

Make the four brownies the hosts and put the money trail beside their work, rather than behind an entrance animation. Visitors meet a memorable cast, then see exactly what feeds them and what staking does and does not promise.

The panel's choice

  1. A Kitchen With Receipts: 52 points over 6 judges, named best by 3
  2. Kitchen counter, open ledger: 50.5 points over 6 judges, named best by 3
  3. The Kitchen Ledger: 37 points over 6 judges
  4. Brownie pantry, live ledger: 33.5 points over 6 judges
  5. Kitchen counter layout: 29.5 points over 6 judges
  6. The Working Kitchen: 28 points over 6 judges

What the judges warned about

GPT-6 Astra: Verify backend authentication changes before describing them as available, and ensure 44-pixel tap targets plus wrapping code and contract addresses at 375 pixels. DeepSeek V4 Pro: The kitchen illustration must not soften the tax, staking risk, and contract receipts; keep those sections as visually serious as the playful mascots. Qwen 3.8 Max: The kitchen illustration must not make the tax explanation, risk note and contract table feel casual; give those sections the same visual weight and authority as the character introductions.

Beside the words, arrange the existing four mascots around a flat, illustrated kitchen counter, with their names and jobs as simple captions. A visible note beside the staking button reads: 'Payouts depend on trading volume and are not guaranteed. BROWNIE can lose value. SUGAR is AI access, not cash.' Link to terms.html. Keep the existing mark at the upper left, a short navigation at the upper right, and Open app as the utility action.
