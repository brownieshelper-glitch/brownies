A new version of the home page: "The Brownies Kitchen Counter"

This version makes the brownies' work and the money that supports it understandable before asking visitors to stake. Visitors keep the warmth and oversized characters they know, but encounter clear funding labels, visible risks and usable evidence instead of repeated cards and a blocking entrance.

Read this first

The judges scored the page as it is today at 6.6 out of 10 and the new version at 6.7, from screenshots, on the same scale.

The signature move

Turn the hero's lower half into one continuous kitchen counter, with Fudge, Crumb, Nib and Chip at their existing large scale, using their original megaphone, headset, magnifier and wrench. Its orange icing edge becomes the top of a dark counter fascia containing the four named jobs, so the opening illustration and team introduction form one scene instead of repeating the mascots in cards.
