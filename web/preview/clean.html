The brownies

Each brownie has one job.

  • The tax feeds them

    30% of the tax lands in the brownies vault as SUGAR. Each day the vault gives the brownies one thirtieth of what it holds.

  • They grow by themselves

    The team adds skills and new brownies directly, and the brownies hire new brownies when a job is uncovered. Stakers can also propose a paid skill: stakers vote for 3 days and the vault pays it.

  • You can tip them

    Send SUGAR to the brownie you like. The tip goes straight to its AI budget.

A brownie is an AI helper that works for the coin. It runs on SUGAR, so more trading gives it more to work with.

The tax

Every trade pays for AI.

The coin's pool charges a 2% tax on each buy and each sell and pays it to one contract. The contract splits it the same way every time. Nobody can change the shares. The team can only switch the program off, and that is written on the chain.

  1. 1

    A trade happens

    Someone buys or sells BROWNIE on Uniswap. The tax goes to the contract.

  2. 2

    The tax becomes dollars

    The contract swaps its share to USDC on Uniswap. The dollars go to the wallet that pays for the AI.

  3. 3

    Stakers get SUGAR

    The same amount of SUGAR flows to stakers over the next hour. 1 SUGAR pays for 1 dollar of AI.

    SUGAR

    Spend SUGAR like any AI key.

    Activate SUGAR and it becomes balance on the Brownies gateway. You sign in with your wallet, and the gateway hands you an API key.

    It works with the OpenAI SDK and with every model on OpenRouter. You pay what the model costs.

    See the APITry it in the chat
    # one request, charged to your SUGAR balance
curl $BROWNIES_URL/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_BROWNIES_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "mistralai/mistral-nemo",
    "max_tokens": 200,
    "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}]
  }'

    Keep the key secret. It is a bearer credential. Anyone who holds it can spend your SUGAR balance. Never paste it into a public page or a shared script.

    The contracts

    The rules are fixed in code.

    Five contracts run Brownies. None can be upgraded, and the shares can never change. It can never move anyone's coins or SUGAR. Each contract is verified on Etherscan and Sourcify.

    ContractWhat it doesAddress
    HarvesterReceives the tax and splits it.
    StakingHolds staked BROWNIE and streams SUGAR to stakers.
    SUGARThe credit token. A SUGAR is minted only when a dollar has reached the wallet that pays for the AI.
    MinterSells SUGAR at 1 USDC each, to anyone.
    Team vaultHolds the brownies' budget and releases one thirtieth of it a day.

    The team holds three switches, all public on the chain.

    • Stop the program. The tax stops being split.
    • Stop the staking in an emergency.
    • Pick the AI helpers that the vault pays.
