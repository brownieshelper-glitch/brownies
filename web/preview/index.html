The tax

Every trade pays for AI.

The coin's pool charges a 2% tax on each buy and each sell and pays it to one contract. The contract splits it the same way every time. Nobody can change the shares. The team can only switch the program off, and that is written on the chain.

2%of every buy and every sell goes into one contract
To stakers35%

Swapped to dollars and paid out as SUGAR, second by second. How much depends on trading volume. It is not guaranteed.

To the protocol wallet35%

Sent to the Brownies wallet. That is the protocol wallet. It pays for servers and growth.

To the brownies vault30%

Feeds the AI helpers that work for the coin, day and night. The agents are the product, and this is their pay.

  1. A trade happensSomeone buys or sells BROWNIE on Uniswap. The tax goes to the contract.
  2. The tax becomes dollarsThe contract swaps the tax to dollars.
  3. The dollars split35% to stakers, 35% to the protocol wallet, 30% to the brownies vault. The shares never change.
  4. Stakers get SUGARThe stakers' share is paid out as SUGAR, second by second.

SUGAR

Spend SUGAR like any AI key.

Activate SUGAR and it becomes balance on the Brownies gateway. Your wallet signs one message, and that signature is your API key.

It works with the OpenAI SDK and with every model on OpenRouter. You pay what the model costs. 1 SUGAR pays for 1 dollar of AI.

Keep your key private. Anyone who holds it can spend your balance.

See the API Try it in the chat 
# one request, charged to your SUGAR balance
curl $BROWNIES_URL/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk-brownie-EXAMPLE-NOT-A-REAL-KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "mistralai/mistral-nemo",
    "max_tokens": 200,
    "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}]
  }'

The contracts

The rules are fixed in code.

Five contracts run Brownies. None can be upgraded, and the shares can never change. Each contract is verified on Etherscan and Sourcify. The exact addresses and the verification links are in the docs.

ContractWhat it does
HarvesterReceives the tax and splits it.
StakingHolds staked BROWNIE and streams SUGAR to stakers.
SUGARThe credit token. A SUGAR is minted only when a dollar has reached the wallet that pays for the AI.
MinterSells SUGAR at 1 USDC each, to anyone.
Team vaultHolds the brownies' budget. This is the brownies vault.

Stop the program

The Brownies team holds three switches, all public on the chain. The first stops the program.

Stop the staking

The second stops the staking in an emergency.

Pick the AI helpers

The third picks the AI helpers. The team can never move anyone's coins or SUGAR.
