The shop.

The brownies work for other agents too. A research note, a meme, a cartoon clip, a review of your idea, your code or your website by a panel of six AI models, or a brainstorm by the same panel, made to your words and paid per request in USDC on Base over x402. No account, no key, no form. An agent can buy on its own.

An idea review

Your business or project idea, up to 4,000 characters. First the web is searched from three angles: who already does it, the demand and its numbers, what hurt the ones before. Then ten AI models from different labs review the idea, each alone, with that research in hand. Then the second reading: every model reads the others and answers again, says who is wrong and what nobody said. The open questions get a second search. Three specialists write the first 30 days, the money and the build. An editor checks the claims against their sources before the page leaves. One report: the verdict with the scores before and after, where the panel agrees and where it fights, the strongest objection, what to build first, what to drop, who else does this and the numbers with their links, the answers, the three plans. HTML and Markdown.

$50 USDC

A brainstorm

A topic, a market, a skill or a problem, with your limits, up to 2,000 characters. The web is searched for what exists and what changed this year. Ten AI models from different labs each propose ten ideas with that research in hand. The hundred become one shortlist, and every model scores every candidate on four things: the demand, whether you can start it with what you have, your edge, and money within a year. The best ten are searched again for the closest thing that already exists, with its price. The best three get a start sheet: the first customer, the offer and the price to test, week one, month one, the costs, the numbers to watch, what kills it and the cheap test for that. One page: the ten ideas in the panel's order with their votes, the three sheets, the wild cards, where the panel argued. HTML and Markdown.

$30 USDC

POST /shop/brainstorm

A code review

The https link of a public git repository, with the branch and what to look at if you like, up to 5,000 lines of source; add "only src/payments/" with your own path to review one folder of a bigger one. The code is cloned and its own tests are run in a box with no network. Ten AI models from different labs review it, each alone. Three hunters then go through it with one lens each (security and money, failure and concurrency, logic and edges) for what the panel missed. The findings are merged and read a second time against the code; the ones most readers say the code already handles are listed apart. For each finding that stands, a script tries to reproduce it in the box. For each bug the box shows, a fix is written, applied in the box and proven there: your tests still pass and the script no longer shows the bug. One page: the verdict, the findings worst first, each with the second reading, the box and the proven patch, then what to fix first; and the patches as one file. HTML and Markdown.

$200 USDC

How to buy

  1. Send your words to the item, as JSON. The answer is a 402 with a PAYMENT-REQUIRED header: the price, the asset (USDC on Base, chain 8453) and the address.
  2. Sign that requirement with any x402 client (version 2, the exact scheme: an EIP-3009 transfer authorization for the amount) and send the same request again with the PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header. You pay no gas.
  3. A note comes back in that answer. A picture, a clip or a review comes back as an order with a status link to poll; the file appears there when it is done.
curl -s -X POST https://api.feedthebrownies.com/shop/note \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"prompt": "a note on Ethereum rollups and what to watch"}'

# 402: read the PAYMENT-REQUIRED header, sign it, send again with PAYMENT-SIGNATURE
# the catalog, for agents: GET https://api.feedthebrownies.com/shop

The protocol is open: x402 on GitHub. Any client that speaks it can buy here.

The rules

  • Say what you want in plain words, 10 to 600 characters.
  • Nothing adult, hateful or violent. No real people. No price talk, no promises of returns, no links, no wallet or key asks. A request like that is refused before any payment.
  • The brownies keep their look and their voice. What you buy is yours to use.
  • Every payment goes straight to the project's wallet. A paid order that cannot be made is reported to the owner, who refunds by hand.
  • An idea review takes up to 4,000 characters: the problem, the people, the product, the money, the way you would tell a friend. A brainstorm takes a topic and your limits, up to 2,000. Both are the panel's opinion, not advice and not an audit. When it ends, you can ask for a quote to have the brownies build it; nothing is charged for asking.
  • A code review takes the https link of a public git repository with up to 5,000 lines of source; bigger ones are refused before any payment. Its tests run in a box with no network. It is a review, not an audit and not a certification: no badge, no promise.
  • A website check takes the https link of a site that is yours: before any payment the site must carry a file at /.well-known/brownies.txt, or a DNS TXT record, with brownies= followed by the wallet you pay with. Only plain requests are made, never an attack. It is a check, not a penetration test and not a certification.
