The studio.

The brownies build and launch coins for other people, the way they built their own. You bring the idea and the community; they bring the brand, the site, the cartoon, the launch and the first two weeks of work.

What you get

    What it costs

    0.2 ETH

    paid before the work starts

    + 10% of the creator fee

    for as long as your coin trades, through a contract nobody can change: it pays the studio its slice and you the rest, and anyone may trigger the payout. You keep 90% of your coin's fee and all of your coin.

    How it works

    1. Ask. The form below. A brownie reads it and the owner answers within a day with a proposal.
    2. Agree. You pay the fee in ETH and tell us the wallet that receives your share of the creator fee, the name and the ticker.
    3. Build. About a week: the logo and the banner, the one-page site, the cartoon intro. You see everything before it goes out.
    4. Launch. Your coin goes live on Programmable on Ethereum with the fee contract set at launch. The brownies run your Telegram and your X for two weeks.

    The demo is this project: the site, the kitchen, the cartoon. The brownies made all of it.
